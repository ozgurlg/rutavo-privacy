Privacy policy

Rutavo

Developer: Özgür Elçi
Contact for privacy questions: ozgurlgfb@gmail.com
Effective date: 27 November 2026

Summary

Rutavo keeps your vehicles, service records and reminders on your phone. It has no account. It does not send your records, notes, amounts, odometer readings or photos to the developer or to anyone else. The app goes online for three things: error reports, which you can switch off in Settings; in the free Android version, one banner ad from Google; and purchases, which your phone's app store and our purchase provider RevenueCat handle. The iPhone version shows no ads. There are no analytics.

What the app is

A log for the vehicles you own: fuel and charging, services, other costs and maintenance reminders. Its estimates are based on what you enter and are not professional advice.

What is stored, and where

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage: your vehicles (names, type, units, odometer), your records (dates, amounts, odometer readings, service types, places, notes), reminders and settings, receipt photos you attach (Pro), whether Pro is owned, one safety copy of the data kept before a data upgrade, and, after a restore, a copy of your previous data so you can undo it. None of this is sent to the developer. Whether Pro is owned is also stored on the phone and is not part of your backup file.

No account

There is no sign-in and no developer server. The only server that holds anything about you for the developer is RevenueCat's, for purchases.

Error reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes or hits an error, it can send a technical report to Sentry, our error-tracking provider: error message and stack trace, app version, operating system and version, device model, a random event ID. Sentry's software may also assign a random ID once per app installation. Reports contain none of your records, notes, amounts or photos. When a report is sent, Sentry receives your phone's IP address as part of the connection and may keep it with the report. Reports are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Sentry stores reports in the European Union; Sentry's own staff and service providers may also access them from other countries, listed at https://sentry.io/legal/subprocessors/. Transfers outside the EU rely on an EU adequacy decision where one applies (for the United States, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, in which Sentry participates) and otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Sentry's Data Processing Addendum. Purpose: finding and fixing bugs, especially anything that could damage your data. Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): legitimate interest. Sentry's privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/. This is on by default and you can switch it off any time in Settings (Settings > "Send crash reports"). These reports are used only to diagnose errors, not to analyse how you use the app (Google Play's data form classes error diagnostics under its "Analytics" purpose; we describe it here in plain words so the two do not seem to disagree). There is no product analytics or usage tracking.

Ads (Android, free version only)

The free Android version shows one banner ad, provided by Google AdMob, and only on the History screen, after your first three days. Ads are non-personalised. To show an ad, Google's software receives technical details such as your IP address (which indicates approximate location), device identifiers (but not the advertising ID: the app does not request it), app interactions and diagnostics, and uses them to deliver and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. Google handles that data under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. On Android the app checks with Google's consent service, before the first ad, whether a consent form is needed, which sends Google a request in every region. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland the app first shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and starts the ad software only if you allow ads. Where that form applies you can change your choice later under Settings > "Ad consent". Your records and photos are never sent to the ad provider. Rutavo Pro removes ads. The iPhone version shows no ads and does not start any ad software.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store; the app has no payment screen of its own. The store handles payment and keeps the purchase record under its own policy; the developer receives no payment card details. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to check that a purchase is valid and to unlock Pro. The app contacts RevenueCat only when you open the Pro screen, buy or restore a purchase, or show your purchase ID in Settings; when the app starts, if Pro is already unlocked on this phone or a payment you started is still pending, to check the purchase; and after that, while the app stays open, to keep the purchase status up to date. If you never do any of these, nothing is sent to RevenueCat. When it does, the app sends RevenueCat a random app ID, the store receipt or purchase token, the product ID and price, your device brand and model, operating system, app version, the country of your app store account, language setting and currency code, and RevenueCat's servers see your IP address. The app ID is random and is not linked to your name or email. It is not necessarily new for every installation: on Android, Google keeps a copy in your Google account's backup, so after you reinstall the app or move to a new phone with the same Google account, the app can pick up the same ID again and your purchases are recognised; on iPhone a reinstall may create a new ID, and "Restore purchases" then links it to the earlier one through your App Store purchase. RevenueCat then asks the store to confirm the purchase. It does not receive your name, email address, vehicle records or payment card details, and we do not ask it to collect your advertising ID. RevenueCat's servers are in the United States, so the data is transferred there. This exchange is needed to buy and restore Pro and cannot be switched off. Legal basis: carrying out the purchase you ask for and keeping a purchased Pro working (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). RevenueCat gives no fixed retention period for this data. To have RevenueCat's record deleted, email us the purchase ID shown under Settings > Privacy > "Purchase ID". We delete it within one month. Your Pro purchase itself stays with your app store account, and you can unlock Pro again with "Restore purchases". RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Whether Pro is owned is also kept on your phone, so Pro works offline once it has been checked.

Reminders and notifications

Scheduled on your phone; the app asks for notification permission only when you turn a reminder on. A reminder can show your vehicle's name and the service due, and may appear on a locked screen depending on your settings.

Photos and PDF (Pro)

Receipt photos you take with the camera or choose from your gallery are resized, stripped of location data and stored in the app's private storage; they are never uploaded. The app asks for camera permission only when you choose "Camera", never for the microphone, and opens your gallery only when you add a photo. A PDF service history is created on your phone. When you share a PDF, a backup file or a CSV export, a temporary copy stays in the app's cache folder until your next share (or until the phone clears the cache), so the receiving app can read it; a backup copy includes all your data and receipt photos.

What you can share yourself

Backup file, CSV export and PDF history go only where you choose to share or save them; the receiving app handles them under its own policy. A PDF includes your receipt photos only if you choose "Include receipt photos" in the PDF options; a backup file always includes them. Backup import and CSV import read a file you choose; nothing is uploaded.

Phone backups by Google and Apple

Your phone may back up the app's database, and the purchase provider's cache (random app ID, Pro status), to your Google or Apple account under their policies; the developer has no access. On Android, receipt photos are excluded from the cloud backup because of its size limit, but are included in your own backup file and in a device-to-device transfer.

Other apps and links

Links open in your browser or mail app under their own rules. If you email support, the developer receives your address and message and uses them only to reply.

Children

For adults; not directed at children.

Retention and deletion

Records stay on your phone until you remove them; uninstalling deletes them. Backups, exports and phone-backup copies stay with you or whoever you gave them to. Error reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days. Reports carry no name or account, so the developer may not be able to find the reports of one particular device. If you want to ask about error reports, write to ozgurlgfb@gmail.com and we will do what is technically possible.

Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

You can see, correct, export and delete your records in the app; for error reports and ads see above; you may switch error reports off in Settings, change your ad choice under Settings > "Ad consent" where the consent form applies (EU/UK/CH, Android), and complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey the KVKK).

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page is updated and the effective date changes. Changes that add data collection will be described here before they reach the app.
